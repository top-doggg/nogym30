No-Gym 30 — Free Email Setup Guide

This is a zero-cost email setup flow using Google Drive + Gmail. No new service required.

Step 1 — Prepare the sample

Create a PDF or Google Doc with the free 3-Day Sample content from your marketing files.

Upload it to Drive and set the link to Anyone with the link → Viewer.

Step 2 — Create a Google Form

Go to Google Forms → Blank form:

Title: No-Gym 30 — Get Your Free 3-Day Sample

Question: “Email address” → Short answer → Required

Open Responses → Link to Sheets.

Step 3 — Turn the form into a landing form on your site

You can collect emails via simple `

` on your landing page.

This redirects submissions from your website into your Google Sheet with no extra tool.

Step 4 — Send emails

Use your existing Gmail. Send to the collected list manually or via a free Google Apps Script merge.
