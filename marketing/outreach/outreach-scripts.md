# No-Gym 30 — Direct Outreach Scripts

## Cold DM template (Instagram / X / Messenger)
“Hey, saw your post about struggling to fit in workouts with a busy schedule.
I built a simple 30-day bodyweight plan for exactly that. $17, instant access, 7-day guarantee.
https://nogym30.netlify.app/ — no pressure, just thought it was relevant.”

## Cold email template
Subject: Saw your post about “no time for fitness”
Body:
Short and relevant only if they actually posted about fitness struggles.
One paragraph. One link. No follow-up unless they reply.

## Comment-first outreach
Find posts from people complaining about:
- gym membership waste
- workout plan too long
- too tired after work
- no time with kids
Comment with empathy, then drop one value line. No link in the comment.
Reply to their reply with the link.

## Follow-up rule
Never follow up more than twice.
Never sell in first message.
First message: value. Second message: link if they engage.
