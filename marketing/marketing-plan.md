# No-Gym 30 Marketing Plan

Goal: Drive buyers to https://nogym30.netlify.app at $17 launch price.

## Core Angles
- "No gym. No equipment. No long workouts."
- Short workouts for real schedules.
- Built for beginners who keep restarting.

## Channels
- Email sequence
- Social posts
- paid ad copy
- Organic / communities

## Tracking
- Add GA4 / Plausible to index.html
- Track: views, CTA clicks, checkout start
- UTM-ready link structure

## Launch Sequence
1. Tease the problem: 3 days before launch
2. Open cart: launch day
3. Reminder: 3 days before close
4. Last call: 12 hours before close
5. Close cart reminder: final

## Price Anchoring
- Lead with $17 launch price
- Anchor against $27 regular and app subscriptions ($9.99–$29.99/month)
- Mention instant digital access
