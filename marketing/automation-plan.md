# No-Gym 30 — Free Automated Marketing Stack (Windows-ready)

## Goal
Run marketing on autopilot using free tools you can log into right now.
Zero paid subscriptions. Zero complex setup.

## Tools
| Tool | Why | Platform |
|------|-----|----------|
| **Meta Business Suite** | Free. Schedule Facebook + Instagram posts from your personal account | https://business.facebook.com |
| **Buffer Free Plan** | Free. Schedule X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok | https://buffer.com |
| **Google Business Profile** | Free. Appears on Google Search + Maps | https://business.google.com |
| **Google Sheets** | Free. Simple posting calendar / checklist | Already have |
| **Microsoft Copilot (Free)** | Free AI writer for captions | Login with Microsoft account |
| **PayPal Checkout** | Already live | — |

## Setup Order
1. Meta Business Suite — 15 minutes
2. Buffer Free Plan — 5 minutes
3. Google Business Profile — 10 minutes
4. Posting calendar — 5 minutes

## Automation Schedule
7 posts per week × 4 weeks = 28 posts total.
Schedule 2 weeks in advance. Revisit every 2 weeks to add more.

| Day | Time | Platform | Angle |
|-----|------|----------|-------|
| Mon | 8 AM | FB | Problem |
| Tue | 7 PM | IG | Proof |
| Wed | 12 PM | X | Short |
| Thu | 9 AM | FB | CTA |
| Fri | 1 PM | IG | How-to |
| Sat | 10 AM | FB | FAQ |
| Sun | 6 PM | X | Brief review |

## Daily Routine (5 minutes)
1. Check comments
2. Share 1 new post in 1 group
3. Reply to 2 messages

## Weekly Routine (15 minutes)
1. Review Buffer schedule
2. Swap in fresh angles from `social-captions.md`
3. Reorder cadence so the same hook never repeats
4. Check GBG insights

## Launch Sequence (Weeks 1–4)
- Week 1: Ramp. 5 posts/week across FB/X
- Week 2: Social proof. Tease quotes
- Week 3: Urgency bump. Price reminder daily
- Week 4: Final close. “Last chance at $17”
